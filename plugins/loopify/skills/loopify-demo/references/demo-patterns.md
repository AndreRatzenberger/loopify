# Demo Patterns

Good demos make the loop visible.

## Normal App Build

Seed a small app with a visible missing behavior and failing acceptance test.

## Research Cleanup

Seed a note with unsupported claims and a source-audit checklist.

## Stuck Loop Rescue

Seed a trace with repeated failure and show loopify-debug changing the
diagnostic.

## Rules

- Keep the target understandable in under two minutes.
- Make the first failure intentional.
- Keep private context out of the material pack.
- Show the trace as the receipt.
